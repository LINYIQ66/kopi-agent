A Century of Singapore Kopi

Kopi, derived from the Malay word for "coffee", is the most iconic traditional beverage of Singapore and Malaysia. Deeply roasted Robusta beans are repeatedly brewed through a unique cotton sock filter, extracting a richer, fuller-bodied taste than regular coffee.

"Siew Dai" is Cantonese for "less sugar". In Singapore's coffee culture, this is the best choice for health-conscious drinkers who refuse to sacrifice flavor. It preserves the rich Robusta taste while cutting the cloying sweetness — every sip returns to coffee's true essence.

In Singapore's Hawker Centres and traditional kopitiams, from dawn to afternoon, you'll see people holding a cup of Kopi with soft-boiled eggs and kaya toast, savoring that simple yet warm Nanyang flavor.
